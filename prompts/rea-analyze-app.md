# Prompt: analyze an app with REA

Use REA to analyze <app or binary>. I have permission to analyze it.

1. Identify the app's network endpoints, protocols, and data formats. Show the evidence for each one.
2. List the features you could observe, and the ones you could not.
3. Write your findings to `findings.md` with sections: Endpoints, Data formats, Auth, Open questions.
4. Do not copy proprietary assets or keys into the output.
